Our Story

Real Tacos.
Real Bay.

Born from the Bay, Tacos De La Bay brings authentic Mexican street food straight to the heart of San Jose. Every tortilla crafted with care, every plate packed with bold, fresh flavors — and every customer treated like family.

Find us at our permanent spot at 2nd & San Fernando in downtown San Jose, or let us roll up to your next big event anywhere across the San Francisco Bay Area.
